📡 Typical Flow

Design (2h)
  ↓
Masks (1 week) ← order externally
  ↓
Wafer prep (4h)
  ↓
Litho × 6 (2 days) ← iterate per layer
  ↓
Doping × 2 (16h) ← n-type & p-type
  ↓
Metal × 3 layers (3 days)
  ↓
Anneal (4h)
  ↓
Test (2h/batch)
  ↓
Package (external or DIY)

⏱️ Timeline

  • First run: 5 weeks (includes mask order)
  • Repeat runs: 2 weeks (masks reusable)
  • Active work: ~40 hours per batch
  • Waiting: Mostly diffusion & shipping
