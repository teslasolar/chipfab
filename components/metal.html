Deposition Methods

  • Sputtering ($3K): Best quality, uniform
  • Evaporation ($1K): Budget option, OK quality
  • Electroplating: Vias only, cheapest

Patterning

  • Spin PR on metal
  • Expose with mask
  • Develop photoresist
  • Wet etch Al (H3PO4 based)
  • Strip remaining PR

Cost

💰 Evaporator: $1K | Sputterer: $3K | Chemicals: $50

Key Parameters

  • Sheet resistance: 0.05Ω/sq target
  • Step coverage: 50%+ (conformal)
  • Via resistance: <1Ω per contact
