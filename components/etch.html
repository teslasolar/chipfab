class Etch:
  def etch(self, wafer, depth):
    expose(HF | SF6)
    wait(t)

Specs

  • Wet: HF/HNO3 solution
  • Dry: SF6 plasma (optional)
  • Rate: 100nm/min typical
  • Selectivity: 10:1 oxide:Si

Cost

💰 Wet: $200 | Plasma: $1K

Safety ⚠️

  • HF requires Ca-gluconate kit
  • Fume hood mandatory
  • PPE: Face shield, gloves
