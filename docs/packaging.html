Process Flow

  • 1. Die separation (dicing saw)
  • 2. Die attach (epoxy to leadframe)
  • 3. Wire bonding (Al or Au, 25µm)
  • 4. Encapsulation (epoxy molding)
  • 5. Final test & marking

DIY vs Outsource

  • DIY Wire Bonding ($5K):
  • West-Bond 7400 series
  • Learning curve steep
  • Good for prototypes
  • Outsource ($5/chip):
  • Professional assembly houses
  • MOQ: 25-100 chips
  • Better quality & speed

Equipment Needed (DIY)

  • Dicing saw: $3K (or scribe & break)
  • Wire bonder: $5K used
  • Die attach: manual pick & place
  • Epoxy cure oven: $500
  • Total: ~$9K

Recommendation

Garage setup: Outsource packaging at $5/chip

Pro setup: In-house with wire bonder

Sources

  • Packages: DigiKey, Mouser, ChipQuik
  • Assembly: Carsem, SPIL (MOQ 100+)
  • Wire bonder: eBay used market
