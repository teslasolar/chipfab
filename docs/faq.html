General

Is this actually possible?

Yes! Sam Zeloof and Jeri Ellsworth have proven garage fabs work. 180nm is achievable with $6K equipment.

Is it legal?

Yes, with proper permits: light industrial zoning, EPA hazmat disposal, OSHA compliance. Budget $2K/year for safety/insurance.

Can I make money?

Potentially. At 60% yield and $500/chip retail, margins are 96%. Key is finding buyers for custom RISC-V chips.

Technical

Why 180nm not smaller?

180nm achievable with UV lithography ($700). Sub-100nm needs DUV ($100K+). Diminishing returns for hobby fab.

Why RISC-V?

Open ISA, no licensing fees, excellent toolchain (GCC, LLVM), growing ecosystem. Alternative: 8051, AVR cores.

What about yield?

Garage: 60% realistic. Pro: 85%+. University tools: 90%. Yield improves with experience and process control.

Safety

How dangerous is HF?

VERY. Penetrates skin, causes deep tissue damage. MANDATORY: fume hood, Ca-gluconate gel, training. No shortcuts.

What about toxic gases?

POCl3/BBr3 for doping require sealed tube furnace with scrubber. Never open hot. Proper ventilation critical.

Economics

What's the real total cost?

Garage: $6K equipment + $2K safety + $235/batch materials = $8.2K to first chip. Pro setup: $50K all-in.

How long to profit?

At 100 chips/month ($50K revenue), equipment pays back in <1 month. Real challenge is sales/distribution.

Can I do this part-time?

Yes. First run takes 5 weeks (much waiting). Repeat runs 2 weeks. Few hours active work per day.

Alternatives

Should I just use MOSIS/Efabless?

For pure design work, yes. $10K for multi-project wafer. But you won't own the means of production.

What about FPGAs?

Faster development, but recurring cost, not custom silicon. LocalChip is for learning + actual chip ownership.
